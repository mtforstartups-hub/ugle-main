## 2. Licence keys and activation

A paid licence key entitles you to activate the Licensor's distributed builds on the number of
machines stated at purchase, for the term stated at purchase. Activation binds a key to a
machine fingerprint stored locally on that machine.

Expiry of a paid term does not delete your data, your media, or your generated transcripts and
indexes. It restricts the features the Licensor's builds gate behind an active licence.

### 2.1 Periodic licence check

Ugle verifies your licence offline, against a signed licence record stored on your machine. That
record is issued for a limited period. While your licence is active, the software refreshes the
record automatically in the background when it is next started with an internet connection
available. You do not need to do anything, and you are not required to be online to use Ugle
day to day.

If the stored record has not been refreshed before it lapses — for example because the machine
has been offline for an extended period — Ugle restricts the features gated behind an active
licence until it is next started with a connection. The software warns you in advance of that
happening. Nothing is deleted, and access is restored as soon as the record is refreshed.

The refresh sends only your licence key and the machine identifier already bound to it. It never
transmits your media, transcripts, search queries, or file names. See also section 4.

The Licensor may revoke a licence that has been refunded, charged back, or used in breach of this
agreement. Revocation takes effect on that machine when its stored record next lapses or is
refreshed.
